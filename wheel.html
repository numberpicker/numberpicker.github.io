Frequently asked questions

What is a number picker wheel?

A number picker wheel is a visual random number generator that spins a wheel with numbers around it. When you spin, the wheel slows down and stops on a random number. It’s a fun, transparent way to make a fair choice.

Is the random number wheel truly random?

Yes! It uses your browser's built-in cryptographic random API (crypto.getRandomValues), so each number in your range has an equal chance of being selected.

Can I use the wheel on my phone?

Absolutely. The wheel is designed to work with touch events — just tap or drag to spin, and it’s fully responsive for any screen size.

What does “no-repeat” do?

When enabled, the wheel will not pick the same number twice until all numbers in the range have been used. Great for raffles or calling a list without replacement.
