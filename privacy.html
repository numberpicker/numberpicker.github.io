Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 13, 2026
Your trust is paramount. Learn how we handle information on Number Picker Wheel.

At Number Picker Wheel, accessible from https://numberpicker.github.io/, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that are collected and recorded by Number Picker Wheel and how we use it.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.

1. Pure Local Generation (No Server Data Transfer)

Number Picker Wheel is engineered to be entirely private. All mathematical processing, range configurations, exclusion lists, and number distributions occur exclusively within your web browser using client-side JavaScript. No data regarding your inputs, ranges, or generated numbers is sent to our servers. Your numbers stay entirely yours.

2. Local Storage Usage

Our utility uses your browser's local cache or history framework to remember your last 20 results and settings temporarily. This data is managed locally on your machine and can be completely eliminated at any time by pressing the "Clear" action on the application card dashboard or by clearing your web browser's data storage cache.

3. Log Files

Number Picker Wheel follows a standard procedure of utilizing server log files when hosting static assets. These files log visitors when they access the web application. The data collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser application type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, and referring or exit pages. These points are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable and are leveraged strictly for analyzing traffic trends and managing site infrastructure.

4. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, Number Picker Wheel may utilize standard technical markers commonly known as "cookies" to store basic user interface variables (such as whether you have toggled the app into Digital Picker Mode). These elements are utilized solely to streamline and customize the rendering engine based on your personal viewing profile.

5. Third-Party Links

Our application infrastructure may link out to open repositories, fonts, or frameworks. We hold no governance over the privacy scripts or asset structures managed by third-party services. We advise our users to check the respective privacy terms of those networks upon departure from our application boundaries.

6. Children's Information

Another layer of our protection mechanism is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and legal guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their online activity.

Number Picker Wheel does not knowingly isolate or harvest any Personal Identifiable Information from individuals under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our platform, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately and we will do our best efforts to promptly remove such records from our logs.

7. Consent

By interacting with our random number picking tool platform, you hereby accord your absolute consent to our structural Privacy Policy stipulations and agree to its operational clauses.
